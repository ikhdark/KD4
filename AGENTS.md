## Audits and reporting

Apply audit rules only to requested audits/reviews, not ordinary implementation, debugging or self-review.

- Derive scope, exclusions and detection criteria from the request before investigation/delegation; include them in assignments. No unauthorized categories, exclusions, thresholds, sampling limits or finding caps.
- Cover all requested hunks/affected behavior, subsystems, or full files/consumers as applicable. Only change-specific requests limit findings to new defects. Inventory manifests/modules, including relevant hidden, ignored, generated and local-only paths; cover producers, consumers and execution paths. Track unread work/traversal errors. Prioritization is not reduced coverage; partial coverage needs user-approved limits or a genuine blocker. Reads/hits are not behavioral coverage.
- Historical-survival work accounts for every commit. Check current callers, startup configuration, defaults and gates first. Select changes by requested behavior; inspect artifacts affecting production/evidence. Close clear survivors with source/equivalence evidence; trace uncertainty further. Equivalence proves only the checked property.

Fully read integrated entries and verify original evidence; track unread items separately. Group shared causes/dispositions with counts/references.
- Findings require demonstrated production triggers; performance findings need measured costs. Keep unverified candidates separate, only for an authorized parent/verifier with evidence/reproduction steps. Blocked verification is a coverage gap, not a finding or clean result.
- Put short audit/benchmark `.txt` reports in `C:\Users\kuh\Desktop\KDR\audits\<task>\`, not `.codex/audits`: status/counts, coverage, findings (ID, severity, `path:line`, defect/trigger, evidence, disposition), measured costs, blockers/next actions.
