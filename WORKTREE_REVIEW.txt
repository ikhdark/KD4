Confirmed findings before repair
--------------------------------
F1 (high): The process-root admission reclaimer stores only one global
registration. A newer live app-server instance overwrites an older one, and
dropping the newer guard clears reclamation instead of restoring the still-live
registration. Under the global root cap this can reject admission even though an
older server owns reclaimable inactive roots.

F2 (medium): Runtime cell closure removes the active cell before publishing its
terminal event. A concurrent observer can fall between those operations, see
neither representation, and return MissingCell for a cell that just completed.

No other correctness, security, lifecycle, compatibility, or generated-contract
defect was confirmed in the frozen scope. Validation status is pending repair.
