  If a creation notification is among the lagged messages and the request task records the instance first, recovery will not attach the other initialized connections supplied by the transport. Those clients permanently miss that thread’s turn, item, and status events. A setup failure between `observe` and the requester-only listener attachment can leave even more state unregistered.
