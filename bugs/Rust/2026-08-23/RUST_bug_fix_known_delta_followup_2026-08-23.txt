KD4-KD-FOLLOWUP-003 -- Unbounded terminal persistence repair
Severity: Critical
Status: FIXED/STRENGTHENED IN COMBINED WORKING TREE; integration unverified
Confidence: High for bounded policy and degraded representation
Files: core/src/{tasks/mod.rs,tasks/mod_tests.rs,task_evidence.rs,

Actual: several repair loops polled every 500 ms forever and placed no timeout

drain across many failed repair tasks, and degraded completion semantics.
Remaining limitation: restart-safe exactly-once delivery is impossible when every
durable backend is permanently unavailable unless a third durable outbox exists.
