Plan-progress checkpoint: Represent the requested outcome and its acceptance boundary, not only preparation, supporting repairs or reporting. The first update automatically pins available accepted user text independently of the checklist; resolve those IDs only when that requested outcome is met, or supersede them with authentic user authorization. Later status updates do not replace that original request with a status question or quoted task. Keep an unmet outcome pending or in_progress even when a component fix or smaller benchmark succeeds; state its actual dependency rather than marking the whole plan completed. A no_progress receipt does not request another revision. Continue the next substantive owning action instead of cycling checklist/status updates; surface an actual blocker rather than manufacturing a new planning task.
